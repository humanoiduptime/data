# Contributing evidence and corrections

HumanoidUptime welcomes reproducible corrections and stronger public evidence.

Please open an issue and include:

1. the affected record, deployment or stable ID;
2. the public source URL and publisher;
3. the exact passage, table, page or timestamp supporting the change;
4. what the source establishes and what it does not establish; and
5. any relevant task, site, time-window, fleet, autonomy or denominator limits.

Do not include confidential information, private telemetry, personal data or
third-party files you are not permitted to redistribute. A submitted source is
reviewed under the published HumanoidUptime methodology and is not automatically
accepted. Manufacturer or operator submissions retain their attributed evidence
level.
